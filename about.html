Our Story

From a backyard of tyres and stones to East Africa's leading strength gym.

Since 2014

Built From Stones & Sweat

Strongman Gym started in 2014 when our founder, Coach Joseph Mwangi, began training a handful of friends in his backyard in Kawangware with old lorry tyres, concrete atlas stones and a home-welded yoke. Word spread fast.

Today we train more than 1,200 members in a 1,400 m² facility on Ngong Road — the largest collection of strongman implements in East Africa. Our athletes have represented Kenya across the continent, but we're just as proud of the grandmother who deadlifted her bodyweight at 64.

Our belief hasn't changed: everyone is stronger than they think. We just help prove it.

Our Mission

To make real-world strength accessible to every Kenyan — through expert coaching, world-class equipment and a community that lifts each other up.

Our Vision

To be Africa's leading strength training centre, producing healthier communities and internationally competitive strength athletes.

Our Promise

Coaching first, always. No ego, no intimidation, no gimmicks — just honest training that works and people who care about your progress.

Core Values

The Strongman Code

01 Respect The Iron

Technique before load. Safety before ego. Every rep counts.

02 Show Up

Consistency beats intensity. Strength is built one session at a time.

03 Lift Others

Chalk up, spot, cheer. Nobody here trains alone.

04 Earn It

No shortcuts, no gimmicks. Hard work, honestly measured.

Why Strongman Gym

Not Just A Gym. A Strength Family.

We're not a mirrors-and-treadmills chain. We're a coaching gym built around heavy, functional training — and a community that cheers louder for your first pull-up than for a world record.

  • Kenya's largest collection of strongman implements under one roof
  • Calibrated competition plates, combo racks and deadlift platforms
  • Every new member gets a free coached onboarding & strength assessment
  • Women-only and youth classes with dedicated coaches
  • Secure parking, hot showers, lockers and a nutrition bar

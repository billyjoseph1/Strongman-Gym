How To Pay

Pay With M-Pesa

  1. Go to M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Pay Bill
  2. Business number: 522522
  3. Account number: STRONG + your phone number
  4. Enter the amount, then your PIN, and confirm.
  5. Show the confirmation SMS at reception or send it on WhatsApp.

We also accept Visa/Mastercard, bank transfer and cash at reception.
